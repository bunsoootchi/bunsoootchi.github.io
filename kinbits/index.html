Here are some answers

Questions you might have

Does it need the internet or an account?
No. Kinbits works offline, there's no account, and your save stays on your phone.
What does Bluetooth do?
It lets your Kinbit meet other Kinbits close by. You only use it if you want to.
Will it bother me with notifications?
Only if you turn them on. Then your Kinbit calls you when something big happens, like a hatch, a visitor, or a proposal.
When can I get it?
