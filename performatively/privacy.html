Performatively Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Performatively is an e-reader app for Android by bunsoootchi. In short: the developer never gets any of your data. Everything stays on your phone, unless you turn on progress sync, which keeps your place in each book in your own Google Drive.

What the app doesn't do

  • It only goes online if you turn on progress sync. Without it, nothing leaves your phone.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account to sign up for.
  • It doesn't sell or share any information with anyone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your books stay in the folders you choose. The app only reads them to show them to you.
  • Your reading progress, settings, stickers, and decorated covers are saved in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • Crash reports are saved on your phone only. If the app crashes, you can choose to copy a report and send it to the developer yourself.
  • Backups are a file that you save wherever you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

Progress sync (optional)

Sync is off unless you sign in with Google in the app. If you do:

  • What's saved: where you are in each book, your phone's name (like "Galaxy Z Fold8"), and a code made from each book file so your devices can match the same book.
  • What's never saved: your books, their titles, your stickers, highlights, bookmarks, or anything else.
  • Where it goes: a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Only Performatively can open it. The developer has no server and never sees it.
  • What the app can see in your Drive: only that hidden folder, plus your email address to show which account is signed in. It can't see your other files.
  • Deleting it: tap Delete synced data in Settings, App tab. You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account settings, under Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services.

Google's own privacy policy covers how Google Drive stores files: policies.google.com/privacy.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. The text being read goes to that voice engine on your phone, which is a separate app with its own privacy policy. Performatively only lists voices that work offline.

Permissions

The app asks you to choose your books folders, so it can find your books. To keep reading aloud with the screen off, Android gives the app permission to run a media service while it reads. It also has internet permission, which is only used for progress sync after you sign in. It asks for nothing else.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
