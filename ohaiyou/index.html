FAQ

Questions you might have

Does anything I say leave my phone?

No. Your pet's brain runs right here on your phone, not in the cloud. Your chats, diary pages, and photos stay on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere. Only you can read them, and you can lock the Journal too.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download your pet's brain (Wi-Fi is best). After that, it works offline, even in airplane mode.

Is it ChatGPT?

No. It's a small AI that lives on your phone. It may forget things or mix them up, but it's trying its very best.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Can I back up my Journal?

Yes. A backup is a single file that you save and keep wherever you like. It's yours, and nobody else ever sees it.

What if I'm going through something heavy?

Please also reach out to someone you trust or a local helpline. You matter.
