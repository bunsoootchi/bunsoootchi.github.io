Help test Ohaiyou

Thank you for helping! Ohaiyou is a little pet that lives on your phone, and you can talk to it. Google needs testers to keep it for 14 days before it can go on the Play Store.

Android iPhone

Android

Use the same Google account for every step, the one your Play Store uses.

1

Join the tester group

Open the link and tap Join group, then Join group again.

Open the tester group

If it says you don't have permission to see the content, that's normal. Joining still works.

2

Become a tester

Open the link and tap Become a tester.

Open the testing page

If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group, then try again.

3

Redeem your code

Do steps 1 and 2 first, with the same Google account. If you redeem before that, Google Play shows error PRS-RPCPM-18.

I'll send you a code in a DM. Each code works once, so please don't share it.

Redeem your code

Or in the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Redeem code.

4

Install it

Tap Install. It should be free.

Open Ohaiyou on Google Play

If it still shows a price, don't pay. Message me and I'll help.

5

Keep it for 14 days

Keep the app installed and stay in the group for at least 14 days. That's all Google needs.

iPhone

Get TestFlight

Get TestFlight

Join and install

Join Ohaiyou on TestFlight

Good to know

The first time you open it

Ohaiyou downloads its brain once (about 2.1 GB), so use Wi-Fi and keep the app open. After that, it works offline.

Found a bug, or have an idea?

Tell me anytime. In the app, Help has a Send feedback button, or just message me.

I have more than one Google account

In the Play Store, tap your profile picture and switch to the account you used for the group before you redeem and install.
