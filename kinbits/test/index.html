iPhone

Get it on TestFlight

On an iPhone, you test with Apple's TestFlight app. No code needed.

1

Get TestFlight

Install TestFlight from the App Store. It's free and made by Apple.

New versions show up in TestFlight by themselves. Turn on automatic updates there to always have the newest one.

Good to know

Found a bug, or have an idea?

Tell me anytime, just message me. A screenshot or a screen recording helps a lot.

I have more than one Google account

In the Play Store, tap your profile picture and switch to the account you used for the group before you redeem and install. If your browser keeps opening the wrong account, open the links in an incognito tab.

The links don't work for me

Open them in Chrome or the Play Store app, not inside Messenger or Instagram. Use a personal Gmail too: work and school accounts can't join the group.

I already have Kinbits from a download link

The Google Play version is a separate app, so you can keep both. Your Kinbits family doesn't move over, so you'll start fresh.
