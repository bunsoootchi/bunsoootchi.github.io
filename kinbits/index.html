Questions you might have

Does Kinbits need the internet or an account?
No. It plays offline, there's no account, and your save stays on your phone. Even meeting nearby Kinbits goes straight from phone to phone.
What does Bluetooth do?
It lets your Kinbit meet other players close by. It's up to you: you can turn Nearby off in Settings anytime.
Will Kinbits send me notifications?
Only if you turn them on. Then you'll hear about the big moments, like a hatch, a visitor, or a proposal.
How much will Kinbits cost?
US$2.99 on both the App Store and Google Play.
