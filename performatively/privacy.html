Performatively Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Performatively is an e-reader app for Android by bunsoootchi. In short: the app does not collect, store elsewhere, or share any of your personal data.

What the app doesn't do

  • It has no internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account to sign up for.
  • It doesn't sell or share any information with anyone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your books stay in the folders you choose. The app only reads them to show them to you.
  • Your reading progress, settings, stickers and decorated covers are saved in the app's own storage on your phone.
  • Crash reports are saved on your phone only. If the app crashes, you can choose to copy a report and send it to the developer yourself.
  • Backups are a file that you save wherever you choose.

Uninstalling the app removes everything it saved.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voices. The text being read goes to that voice engine on your phone, which is a separate app with its own privacy policy. Performatively only lists voices that work offline.

Permissions

The app asks you to choose your books folders, so it can find your books. To keep reading aloud with the screen off, Android gives the app permission to run a media service while it reads. It asks for nothing else.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
